COGNITO

AI Readiness Framework for Electric Utilities

A structured, five-step methodology to assess AI readiness, identify appropriate use cases, evaluate risks, and implement safeguards for responsible AI deployment in grid operations.

Learn More

What is COGNITO?

COGNITO is a comprehensive AI Readiness Self-Guide Framework developed by Idaho National Laboratory's Center for Securing Digital Energy Technology (CSDET).

Purpose

Electric utilities face increasing pressure to adopt artificial intelligence for improved operations, but lack a structured approach to assess readiness, identify appropriate applications, and manage risks. COGNITO bridges this gap by providing a systematic framework tailored specifically for grid operators.

Whether you're an investor-owned utility, municipal utility, electric cooperative, public power authority, independent system operator, or regional transmission organization, COGNITO helps you evaluate AI opportunities while ensuring safe, responsible deployment.

Key Objectives

  • Evaluate readiness across technical, organizational, and governance dimensions
  • Identify optimal areas that would benefit from AI adoption
  • Match use cases based on consequence profiles and technology maturity
  • Assess risks and identify appropriate engineering controls
  • Plan implementation with clear scenarios and success metrics

The COGNITO Framework

A systematic five-step process that guides utilities from initial capability assessment through to implementation planning with appropriate safeguards.

01
Identify Business Context

Identify Business Context

Key Questions: Where are we today? What capabilities and gaps define our current readiness for AI?

Map current capabilities to identify where AI can fix critical gaps, optimize processes, or enhance high-performing operations. Document specific opportunities with quantified impacts.

Deliverable: Business maturity analysis, prioritized opportunity list, and AI domain mapping

02
Align AI Use Cases

Align AI Use Cases

Key Questions: What should we tackle first and what are the stakes? Which use cases align with our mission while matching our risk tolerance?

Match opportunities to appropriate AI applications using a comprehensive catalog organized by consequence level (Low, Moderate, High, Highest) and technology readiness (Ready Now, Emerging, Future State).

Deliverable: Selected use cases with consequence profiles and technology maturity assessments

03
AI Principles Analysis

Analyze AI Principles

Key Questions: What do we actually have, what do we need, and what gaps require attention before proceeding?

Explore readiness through structured questions across seven critical principles: Risk Management, Data Availability, Infrastructure, Investment Capacity, Skilled Personnel, Regulatory Compliance, and Clear Objectives.

Deliverable: Readiness assessment with documented capabilities, gaps, and red flags

04
Implementation Planning

Implementation Planning & Readiness Validation

Key Questions: What exactly are we building and how will we implement it? What will success look like?

Develop detailed scenarios connecting current operations to AI-enabled futures. Determine build/buy/partner approach and validate resources are in place to execute.

Deliverable: Implementation scenarios, KPIs, build/buy decision, and resource validation

05
Engineering Controls

Evaluate Engineering Controls & Mitigations

Key Questions: How will we deploy and sustain it safely? What controls and safeguards are required?

Identify appropriate safeguards across engineering, operational, and governance domains based on consequence analysis. Access framework guidance to support responsible deployment.

Deliverable: Control strategy documentation and framework references

Key Features

Comprehensive Use Case Catalog

Pre-vetted AI applications for grid operations, organized by consequence level and technology readiness, with detailed implementation guidance and risk characterization.

Risk Analysis Wizard

Structured consequence scoring across nine categories including safety, service continuity, and asset integrity, with failure mode identification and security threat assessment.

Data Readiness Assessment

Systematic evaluation of data availability, quality, integration complexity, and governance requirements with gap analysis and remediation planning.

Implementation Worksheets

Detailed scenario planning tools that define AI solutions, establish success metrics, and guide build/buy/partner decisions based on organizational capabilities.

Framework Reference Library

Curated guidance on relevant standards including NIST AI RMF, NERC CIP, IEC 62443, and Cyber-Informed Engineering, organized by applicability to your deployment.

Professional Export Options

Generate formatted PDF or text reports suitable for stakeholder review, executive briefings, vendor RFPs, and project charter documentation.

Flexible Assessment Depth

COGNITO offers a two-phase approach allowing organizations to choose their depth of analysis

1

Phase 1: Critical Assessment

Steps 1-2 + Risk Management

Provides sufficient insight for initial decision-making, stakeholder discussions, and go/no-go decisions. Includes business context, use case alignment, and foundational risk analysis.

What You'll Get:

  • Business maturity analysis with capability scores
  • Selected AI use cases with risk profiles
  • Quantified risk scores and initial control recommendations

Phase 2: Deep Analysis

Complete All 5 Steps

Comprehensive implementation readiness with detailed scenario planning, complete principle assessment, and full control framework documentation ready for execution.

Everything from Phase 1, Plus:

  • Detailed implementation scenarios with KPIs
  • Build/buy/partner decision with documented rationale
  • Comprehensive control framework tailored to your deployment

Why Use COGNITO?

🎯

Grounded in Reality

Prevents common AI initiative failures by ensuring use cases align with actual business capabilities, data readiness, and strategic priorities before significant investment.

⚖️

Risk-Proportional Controls

Consequence-driven approach ensures safeguards match the criticality of each application—avoiding both over-engineering low-risk systems and under-protecting critical functions.

Actionable Deliverables

Produces professional documentation suitable for executive briefings, vendor RFPs, board presentations, and project charter development.
